About these projects

My first projects were mostly copy-paste and hope. These are the ones I made while working through HTML, CSS and JavaScript one small idea at a time — a calculator one week, a snake game the next. Some took an evening, some took days of things not working.

I kept them because they are the honest record of how I learned. They have been polished and fixed up so they run properly on any screen, but I did not rewrite them into something fancier than they are.
